Help & Guide

What is this app?

Homerun Practice helps coaches build timed baseball practice plans from a library of 368 drills sourced from the Youth Baseball Canada knowledge base. It runs entirely in your browser — no account, no server, no internet required after the first load. All data is saved locally in your browser.

Getting started in 3 steps:

  1. Fill in the Session Configuration — team, date, theme, and how much time you have.
  2. Search or filter the Drill Library and click + on any drill to add it to your plan (or load a Skill-Focused Plan to start from a template).
  3. Click Save to keep the plan, or Print / PDF to hand it to your team.

📲 Add to your Home Screen

Install this app so it opens full-screen, without the browser bar — just like a native app.

On iPhone/iPad (Safari):

  1. Tap the Share icon in Safari's toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add — the app icon now appears on your Home Screen and opens full-screen, without the browser bar.

On Mac (Safari):

  1. Click the Share icon in Safari's toolbar (or use the File menu).
  2. Choose Add to Dock.
  3. The app now opens from your Dock in its own window, like a native app.

🆕 Version, updates & feedback

The version you are running is shown in the footer at the bottom of every screen — for example v1.0. Version numbers work like this: a small update adds 0.1, and a big update adds 1.0.

Staying up to date: when the app starts, it quietly checks whether a newer version has been published. If there is one, you'll see an Update available message with an Update now button. Updating only refreshes the app files — your saved practice plans are stored on this device and are never touched. If you're offline, the check is skipped and the app works exactly as usual.

What's new: the first time you open a new version, a short summary of the changes appears. You can bring it back any time from the What's new link in the footer.

Full history: open the version history page to see every release, its date, and what changed.

Found a problem, or have an idea? Use the button below (or Send feedback in the footer). Rate the app, pick what your feedback is about, and describe what happened — the app writes the email for you and opens it in your mail app, addressed to the Homerun Baseball Ottawa team.

Practice Planner

  • Search: type in the search box to find drills by name, skill, or tags.
  • Filter by skill: click a skill chip (Hitting, Throwing, Infield, etc.) to narrow the list.
  • Filter by intensity: Low / Medium / High — useful for warm-up vs. main work vs. conditioning.
  • Filter by age: select an age range to see only age-appropriate drills.
  • Drill cards: click any drill card to see the full detail — purpose, setup, coaching cues, execution steps, common faults, and equipment needed.
  • Add to plan: click the + button on a drill card to add it to your practice plan. Click again to remove it.
  • Reorder drills: use the ↑↓ arrows on plan items to rearrange the order.
  • Stations mode: switch the plan to Stations to run several drill groups at once, with a single time allocation per station instead of per drill.
  • Time budget: the bar shows how much of your session time is used up.
  • Skill-Focused Plans: pre-built plans organized by skill — click any plan card to load it instantly.
  • Save / Load: save your plan to browser storage and load it later.
  • Print / PDF: generates a printable practice plan with timeline, drill details, and coach's notes.

Export & Import saved plans

Because your data lives in this browser on this device, it isn't automatically shared with another device or browser.

  • Export: downloads all of your saved plans as a single .json file. Use this to back them up or move them to another device.
  • Import: loads plans from a previously exported file. Plans are added to what you already have — a plan with the same name as one you already saved will be overwritten, but everything else stays untouched.
